Our Services

Explore the services provided by the NWU Library Management System to make library operations easier and more efficient.

Book Management

Add, update, view, and manage book records while keeping track of ISBNs, authors, categories, quantities, and availability.

Book Search

Quickly find books using criteria such as title, author, ISBN, category, and availability.

Borrower Management

Manage borrower information, registration details, borrowing status, and borrowing history in one centralised system.

Book Borrowing

Process book loans efficiently by recording borrowers, books, issue dates, and due dates.

Book Returns

Record returned books, update their availability, and keep loan information accurate and up to date.
